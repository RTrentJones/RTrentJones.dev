I'm Trent, a software engineer in Los Angeles. I studied neuroscience and computer science at USC,
started my career building software for a clinical genetics lab, and now work at Audible after
spending most of my time at Amazon on Alexa.

Outside work I build a fairly mixed collection of things. Sometimes I want a tool that doesn't
exist, as with [HeistMind](/heistmind/). Sometimes implementing an idea seems like the best way to
understand it, as with [pg_kafka](/pg_kafka/). And sometimes the repeated glue around those projects
turns into a project of its own, which is how [Greenlight](/greenlight/) happened.

This site is where I keep those projects and write about how they came together, the bugs I didn't
expect, and whatever I learned along the way.
